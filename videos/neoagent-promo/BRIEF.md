## Intent

A short GitHub promo, not a feature tour. The film opens on the real app already working: its computer, its memory, a schedule. Motion is the camera and the product, with one line of type — the README line — arriving late. Quiet ownership, olive and gold, no neon AI look and no wall of text.

Left behind: a labeled screenshot slideshow, and a status-log film that would have read as generic AI text.

## Assets

- Real Flutter UI captures only. Crop or replace anything that shows a person, message, email, token, path, or account name.
- GitHub social card is a separate 1280×640 still from the same system, solid background, type inside a safe margin.
- Still frames for the README, exported from the same captures.

## Customizations

- Place the real UI captures in the frames; do not rebuild the product in HTML.
- Each scene uses a proven motion treatment, paced across the shot instead of a slideshow fade.
- A design system locks the video to the app palette (deep olive, warm gold, sage) and Geist.
- No voiceover. A quiet music bed is allowed if a local or signed-in source can supply one; otherwise the film stays silent.

## Notes

- Show the product in the first second. Brand name comes at the end.
- One idea only: you host it, and it is already at work.
- Do not invent screens, ratings, or features.
- Dark theme is the primary look. Light theme is not required.
- Banner, stills, and the 16:9 film share one visual system.
